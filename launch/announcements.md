## LinkedIn

How can we turn ideas about cancer bioelectricity into experiments we can actually test?

I’m opening a research preview of Cancer Bioelectricity Explorer, a browser-based tool for exploring the literature and testing simple membrane-circuit hypotheses, including trans-plasma-membrane electron transfer (TPMET).

You can search the literature collection, vary membrane capacitance and ionic conductance, explore electron flux and charge compensation, and export a record of your assumptions and predictions.

This is an exploratory tool. Default parameters are illustrative, cancer-outcome hypotheses are unvalidated, and the literature collection is awaiting screening and is not exhaustive. The equations, assumptions and sources are documented.

I’m looking for colleagues who would be willing to try one simulation, flag a missing paper or discuss a suitable calibration dataset. Feedback on the physics, experimental interpretation and usability would be particularly useful.
